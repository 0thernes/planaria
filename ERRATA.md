## Withdrawal & Retraction Policy

If a fatal flaw is discovered in any central claim (`C01`–`C10`), formal proof, or published preprint:
1. **Immediate Public Notice:** A `RETRACTION` or `CORRIGENDUM` banner will be added to the top of `README.md`, `ERRATA.md`, and the GitHub Release notes within 48 hours of confirmation, without deleting the historical git tag or commit trail.
2. **Preprint & Archive Update:** Any posted arXiv/Zenodo/OSF record will be updated with a superseding version (or formal withdrawal notice if the main claim fails), and `0thernes-research-archive` (`0TH-BUILD-002`) will record the superseding status alongside the immutable original SHA-256 hash.
3. **Credit to Discoverer:** Any external reviewer who identifies a valid counterexample or soundness gap will be credited by name (with consent) in `ERRATA.md` and the superseding manuscript.
